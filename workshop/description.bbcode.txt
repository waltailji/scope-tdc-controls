[h1]Scope TDC Controls[/h1]
Ever been in the middle of a convoy trying to set up successive shots out of your bow and stern tubes, only to be frustrated by the gyro angle receivers taking forever to synchronize when switching directions, having to switch to the TDC view, and getting your scope moved around when you go back to look through it?  Now you can avoid these frustrations by having all the important information and controls on your scope view, setup both torpedo rooms for zero gyro angle shots in the bow and stern directions ahead of time so that you can launch successive torpedoes in a matter of a seconds.  This mod comes with some "cheats" and critical fixes to the stock torpedo fire control behavior as well.  Details below.

[h2]Gyro angle indicators (rolling digit style)[/h2]
Compact mechanical-style counters sit under the bearing tape. Every drum rolls over smoothly, just like a real odometer, including 359 → 000.
[list]
[*][b]TDC[/b] – the gyro angle (Schusswinkel) computed by the TDC
[*][b]Parallax[/b] – the parallax correction the TDC adds (+/-)
[*][b]Bow / Stern[/b] – the angle the tube gyros are actually set to, so you can watch them catch up
[*][b]Gyro-set lamp[/b] – green = tubes set to the TDC angle, red = synchronizing, dark = receiver off
[/list]
If nobody is manning the TDC, the TDC counter greys out with a "TDC not manned" note.
[b]Double-click a Bow/Stern counter[/b] to switch to that receiver: the scope swings round until the TDC angle matches what those tubes are already set to, so they're ready instantly.

[h2]Control panel[/h2]
The "Scope TDC Controls" panel beside the periscope raise/lower control (roll it up to its title bar with the caret):
[list]
[*][b]Target bearing[/b] – Follow / Not follow, with a blue lamp while not following
[*][b]Parallax mode[/b] – Forward or Backward
[*][b]Windows[/b] – check boxes to show or hide the gyro angle counters, TDC dials, firing selector, direction selector and fire lever, plus a button to reset window sizes
[*][b]Settings[/b] drop-down (see below)
[/list]
A button in the scope screen's stock left sidebar (next to the chronometer) shows or hides the panel and all its windows.

[h2]TDC dials[/h2]
Set up your firing solution without leaving the scope: a window holding the TDC's [b]AOB[/b], [b]target speed[/b], [b]target distance[/b] and [b]target length[/b] dials, styled after the real ones.
[list]
[*]Grab a dial and turn it, just like on the TDC, or click a value on its scale; hold Shift for fine adjustment, or use the mouse wheel to step
[*][b]AOB (Lagenwinkel)[/b] – red port / green starboard halves and a window with the rolling ones digits; the TDC follows the dial as you turn it
[*][b]Target speed[/b] – 0–55 kn; [b]Target distance[/b] – 3–100 hm on the TDC's own stretched scale, with the TDC's knob
[*][b]Target length (Streubreite)[/b] – 100–200 m on a turning ring, shown only while a spread is selected
[*][b]Voice callouts[/b] – the crew member at the scope announces what you dial in, with the game's own voice lines
[*]Greyed out and locked while the TDC isn't manned
[/list]

[h2]Firing selector & direction selector[/h2]
The boat's rotary switches, in off-white windows like the real switch boxes, with the boat's own knob clicks:
[list]
[*][b]Firing selector[/b] – Einzelschuß (single tube / spread), Fächerschuß (salvo tubes) and T.Abf. Schalter (firing station); only your sub's positions are offered
[*][b]Direction selector[/b] – Zielrichtung (bearing source: attack periscope, UZO, observation periscope or off) and Schußrichtung (gyro angle receiver: bow, stern or off)
[/list]

[h2]Fire lever[/h2]
A spring-loaded Aus / Schuß lever: press and hold it to fire the selected tubes through the boat's own launch lever. Its ready lamp only turns green when the shot makes sense – hover it to see why it's red:
[list]
[*]no tube selected, or a selected tube not flooded
[*]gyro angle receiver not on the selected tubes' end
[*]target beyond the torpedo's run (the TDC's "max distance to target" drum) – how the watch officer made the system safe
[*]gyro angle past the ±135° stop
[*]dark = no power: the firing station switch is on Off
[/list]
While the lamp is red, the lever does not fire.

[h2]Periscope - Raise fully / lower fully[/h2]
Two buttons above and below the stock periscope raise/lower control run the periscope all the way up or down. Click again (or use a stock raise/lower button) to stop.

[h2]Settings (saved between sessions)[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Show gyro receiver angles / Show active receivers only / Show parallax angle[/b] – Control which gyro and parallax angles you want to see
[*][b]Auto set gyro receiver[/b] – automatically sets the selected gyro angle receiver based on what torpedo tubes are flooded and selected (none = off; bow and stern both checked = the direction your scope is viewing)
[*][b]Auto set parallax mode[/b] – automatically sets parallax mode (forward/backward) based on your selected tube, gyro receiver, or (when none selected) the direction of your scope.
[*][b]Realistic TDC follower[/b] – turn off for the TDC gyro angle calculation to update 10x faster when moving your scope around.
[*][b]Realistic receiver sync[/b] – turn off for your gyro angle receivers to synchronize 10× faster; no more waiting ages for you to use your aft torpedoes.
[*][b]TDC operator required[/b] – turn off to use the TDC without a crew member at it
[*][b]Voice callouts[/b] – crew announcements for values set with the TDC dials
[*][b]Ctrl+drag to move[/b] – off (default): drag a window's background to move it; on: hold Ctrl to move
[/list]

[h2]Fixes to the base game[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Single torpedo launch fix[/b] – fixed a bug where, when in single-fire mode, if you selected (checked) another flooded torpedo tube after firing the first torpedo, the game would launch your second torpedo if you didn't wait long enough.  no more wasted torpedoes!
[*][b]Stern shots from the scope[/b] – the torpedo panel no longer forces the gyro receiver to Bow whenever a bow tube is flooded.
[*][b]Not following holds[/b] – the game no longer turns bearing follow-up back on every frame while a target is locked.
[*][b]Tube selection kept[/b] – hiding or showing the torpedo panel no longer changes which tube is selected, and the fire-control box tube selector stays in step with your choice.
[/list]

[h2]Recommended Torpedo Attack Workflow[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Pick a scope to set up your gyros with (UZO/attack periscope/observation periscope)
[*]Set that scope as your target bearing source
[*]Turn your scope to a point to a relative bearing of 0 degrees
[*]Switch to "Bow" on gyro angle receiver selector and allow the gyros fully synchronize.
[*]Switch to "None" on the gyro receiver selector.
[*]Turn your scope to a point to a relative bearing of 180 degrees.
[*]Switch to "Stern" on the gyro angle receiver selector and allow the stern gyro to fully synchronize.
[*]Select "None" on the gyro receiver selector.
[/olist]

Keeping your gyros set to the correct directions, and leaving the gyro angle receiver selector set to "None" when switching directions or when not on an active attack will help save a lot of time and frustration with making rapid successive torpedo launches in both directions (in the middle of a convoy).  The stock system lead to a lot of unnecessary gyro chasing when performing routine scanning with the scopes and made it difficult to optimize attack planning in both directions.
