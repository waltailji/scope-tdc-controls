[h1]Scope TDC Controls[/h1]
Ever been in the middle of a convoy setting up shots from your bow and stern tubes, only to wait forever for the gyro receivers to synchronize, switch to the TDC view, and come back to a scope that has moved? This mod puts the important information and controls on your scope view, so you can set up both torpedo rooms for zero gyro angle shots ahead of time and fire in quick succession in either direction. It also includes some optional "cheats" and fixes to the stock torpedo fire control. Details below.

[h2]Video Overview[/h2]
To see how I use the mod, watch [url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEUwLaamtOc]my YouTube video[/url].

[h2]Gyro angle digital display[/h2]
Rolling-drum counters (to place around the bearing tape):
[list]
[*][b]TRUE[/b] - where the scope is looking, as a compass bearing (optionally on the binoculars too)
[*][b]TDC Gyro[/b] - the gyro angle the TDC computes; its blue lamp is lit while not following (click it to toggle follow-up)
[*][b]Parallax[/b] - the correction the TDC adds (+/-)
[*][b]Bow / Stern[/b] - the angle the tube gyros are actually set to; the selected receiver's lamp is green when set, red while synchronizing
[/list]
Greyed out when nobody is at the TDC. [b]Double-click a Bow/Stern counter[/b] to switch to that receiver: the scope swings round until the TDC angle matches those tubes. Dial icons beside the counters open the target bearing and gyro setting windows.

[h2]Mod control panel[/h2]
Beside the periscope raise/lower control (roll it up with the caret):
[list]
[*][b]Target bearing[/b] - Follow / Not follow
[*][b]Parallax mode[/b] - Forward / Backward
[*][b]Windows[/b] - show or hide each window, reset their sizes
[/list]
A sidebar button on the scope screen shows or hides it all.

[h2]TDC dials[/h2]
[b]AOB[/b], [b]target speed[/b] and [b]target distance[/b], styled after the real TDC.
[list]
[*]Turn a dial, click a value on its scale, or use the mouse wheel; Shift = fine
[*][b]AOB[/b] - angle on bow, port or starboard, of target; switch to disable TDC's [b]AOB motor[/b]
[*][b]Voice callouts[/b] - the crew member at the scope announces what you dial in (same as called out on stock TDC data entry)
[/list]

[h2]TDC target bearing[/h2]
The Zielrichtung fine (10 deg a turn) and coarse dials: brass needle = bearing source, red flag = the TDC's bearing. Turn or click to set it by hand; the TDC turns to it smoothly so AOB tracking keeps up. With follow-up on, the TDC pulls it back to the needle.

[h2]Torpedo room gyro setting[/h2]
Brass needle = angle received from the TDC, red flag = angle set on the tubes; the game's own auto / manual logic applies.
[list]
[*][b]Bow[/b] - spread angle, gyro coarse / fine, fast sync lever with red / green lamps, salvo size and setting mode switches, hand wheel
[*][b]Stern[/b] - gyro coarse / fine and hand crank
[/list]

[h2]Salvo spread[/h2]
Pops up for spreads (or always, with its check box), anchored beside the TDC dials:
[list]
[*][b]Target length[/b] - the turning ring dial to set the target length to calculate spread over
[*][b]Spread angle[/b] - brass needle = computed angle, red flag = angle set (drag it by hand)
[*][b]Spread motor[/b] switch - on: the spread angle is computed from target length; off: allows manually setting the spread angle directly
[/list]

[h2]Own angular speed[/h2]
Optional TDC rate-of-turn knob (-3 to 3 deg/s) for firing while turning; pops up for a salvo in a turn, on boats whose TDC has it.

[h2]Torpedo tubes[/h2]
SH3-style tube statuses, fore and aft rows: black = empty, grey = loaded, yellow = maintenance, blue = flooded, green = selected.
Click to select, double-click to flood or drain, right-click for Flood / Drain, Select and Launch; Ctrl+click fires a tube with its own launch lever.

[h2]Torpedo settings[/h2]
SH3-style torpedo settings window when a tube is selected:
[list]
[*][b]Depth[/b] - dial from 1 to 14 m in 0.25 m steps
[*][b]Speed[/b] - slow / medium / fast, only the speeds the selected torpedoes have, shown in knots
[*][b]Pistol[/b] - AZ (impact) or MZ (magnetic)
[/list]
With the Torpedo Room mod installed, the settings are stored on each selected torpedo only ([url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3823181610]Torpedo Room[/url] mod recommended but not required).

[h2]Firing & direction selectors[/h2]
The boat's rotary switches, with its own knob clicks:
[list]
[*][b]Firing selector[/b] - Einzelschuss (launch mode), Fächerschuss (salvo mode) and T.Abf. Schalter, laid out as on your boat's box
[*][b]Direction selector[/b] - Zielrichtung (bearing source) and Schussrichtung (gyro angle receiver)
[/list]
A red [b]firing circuit lamp[/b] locks the switches for 4 s after each fire command.

[h2]Launch lever[/h2]
Spring-loaded Aus / Schuss: fires the selected tubes while its lamp is green. Red (tube not ready, wrong receiver, out of range, past the +/-135 deg stop) or dark (firing station off): hover for why.

[h2]Periscope - Raise / lower fully[/h2]
Buttons above and below the stock periscope control run the scope all the way up or down (a setting hides them with the stock control).

[h2]Settings (persist between sessions)[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Show gyro receiver angles / active receivers only / parallax angle[/b]
[*][b]Auto set gyro receiver[/b] - follows the checked, flooded tubes
[*][b]Auto set parallax mode[/b] - follows tube, receiver or scope direction
[*][b]Auto select tube when flooding[/b]
[*][b]Auto display spread settings[/b], [b]Tint panels with sub lighting[/b]
[*][b]Realistic TDC follower / receiver sync[/b] - off = 10x faster TDC and gyros
[*][b]TDC operator required[/b] - off = use the TDC unmanned
[*][b]Voice callouts[/b], [b]Ctrl+drag to move[/b], [b]Hotkey[/b] - click and press a key to show / hide the mod
[/list]

[h2]Fixes to the base game[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Single torpedo launch fix[/b] - a tube checked after pressing fire no longer launches too
[*][b]Stern shots from the scope[/b] - the torpedo panel no longer forces the receiver to Bow
[*][b]Not following holds[/b] while a target is locked
[*][b]Tube selection kept[/b] when hiding or showing the torpedo panel
[*][b]Keep my scope bearing[/b] - a periscope comes back on the bearing you left it at (also after lock / unlock); works alongside Stop Moving My Scope
[/list]

[h2]Recommended workflow[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Set the scope you attack with as the target bearing source
[*]Turn it to 0 deg relative, select "Bow" on the receiver selector and let the gyros synchronize
[*]Turn it to 180 deg relative, select "Stern" and let the stern gyro synchronize
[*]Leave the receiver selector on "None" until you attack
[/olist]
This avoids the stock system's constant gyro chasing and lets you fire quickly in both directions.
