[h1]Scope TDC Controls[/h1]
Ever been in the middle of a convoy setting up shots from your bow and stern tubes, only to wait forever for the gyro receivers to synchronize, switch to the TDC view, and come back to a scope that has moved? This mod puts the important information and controls on your scope view, so you can set up both torpedo rooms for zero gyro angle shots ahead of time and fire in quick succession in either direction. It also includes some optional "cheats" and fixes to the stock torpedo fire control. Details below.

[h2]Gyro angle counters[/h2]
Rolling-drum counters under the bearing tape:
[list]
[*][b]TDC[/b] – the gyro angle the TDC computes; its blue lamp is lit while not following (click it to toggle follow-up)
[*][b]Parallax[/b] – the correction the TDC adds (+/-)
[*][b]Bow / Stern[/b] – the angle the tube gyros are actually set to; the selected receiver's lamp is green when set, red while synchronizing
[/list]
Greyed out when nobody is at the TDC. [b]Double-click a Bow/Stern counter[/b] to switch to that receiver: the scope swings round until the TDC angle matches those tubes.

[h2]Control panel[/h2]
Beside the periscope raise/lower control (roll it up with the caret):
[list]
[*][b]Target bearing[/b] – Follow / Not follow
[*][b]Parallax mode[/b] – Forward / Backward
[*][b]Windows[/b] – show or hide each window, reset their sizes
[/list]
A sidebar button on the scope screen shows or hides the panel and its windows.

[h2]TDC dials[/h2]
[b]AOB[/b], [b]target speed[/b] and [b]target distance[/b], styled after the real TDC.
[list]
[*]Turn a dial, click a value on its scale, or use the mouse wheel; Shift = fine
[*][b]AOB[/b] – red/green halves with rolling ones digits, plus the TDC's [b]AOB motor[/b] switch (AOB tracking)
[*][b]Voice callouts[/b] – the crew member at the scope announces what you dial in
[/list]

[h2]Salvo spread[/h2]
Pops up for spreads (or always, with its check box), anchored beside the TDC dials:
[list]
[*][b]Target length[/b] – the turning ring dial
[*][b]Spread angle[/b] – brass needle = computed angle, red flag = angle set (drag it by hand)
[*][b]Spread motor[/b] switch – on: the TDC drives the flag to the computed angle
[/list]

[h2]Own angular speed[/h2]
Optional window with the TDC's rate-of-turn knob (-3 to 3 °/s), allowed for when firing while turning. Only on boats whose TDC has the knob.

[h2]Torpedo tubes[/h2]
SH3-style circles, fore and aft rows: black = empty, grey = loaded, blue = flooded, green = selected. Click to select or deselect, double-click to flood or drain.

[h2]Torpedo settings[/h2]
An SH3-style window while a tube is selected:
[list]
[*][b]Depth[/b] – dial from 1 to 14 m in 0.25 m steps
[*][b]Speed[/b] – slow / medium / fast, only the speeds the selected torpedoes have, shown in knots
[*][b]Pistol[/b] – impact or magnetic
[/list]
With the Torpedo Room mod installed, the settings are stored on each selected torpedo.

[h2]Firing & direction selectors[/h2]
The boat's rotary switches, with its own knob clicks:
[list]
[*][b]Firing selector[/b] – Einzelschuß, Fächerschuß and T.Abf. Schalter (your sub's positions only)
[*][b]Direction selector[/b] – Zielrichtung (bearing source) and Schußrichtung (gyro angle receiver)
[/list]
A red [b]firing circuit lamp[/b] locks the switches for 4 s after each fire command.

[h2]Launch lever[/h2]
Spring-loaded Aus / Schuß: press to fire the selected tubes. It fires only while its lamp is green (hover for the reason). Red: no tube selected or not flooded, receiver on the wrong end, target out of range, or gyro angle past the ±135° stop. Dark: firing station switch off.

[h2]Raise / lower fully[/h2]
Buttons above and below the stock periscope control run the scope all the way up or down.

[h2]Settings (saved)[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Show gyro receiver angles / active receivers only / parallax angle[/b]
[*][b]Auto set gyro receiver[/b] – follows the checked, flooded tubes (both ends = the way the scope looks)
[*][b]Auto set parallax mode[/b] – follows tube, receiver or scope direction
[*][b]Auto select tube when flooding[/b] – off = flooding leaves your selection alone
[*][b]Auto display spread settings[/b], [b]Tint panels with sub lighting[/b]
[*][b]Realistic TDC follower / receiver sync[/b] – off = 10× faster TDC and gyros
[*][b]TDC operator required[/b] – off = use the TDC unmanned
[*][b]Voice callouts[/b], [b]Ctrl+drag to move[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Fixes to the base game[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Single torpedo launch fix[/b] – a tube checked after pressing fire no longer launches too
[*][b]Stern shots from the scope[/b] – the torpedo panel no longer forces the receiver to Bow
[*][b]Not following holds[/b] while a target is locked
[*][b]Tube selection kept[/b] when hiding or showing the torpedo panel
[/list]

[h2]Recommended workflow[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Set the scope you attack with as the target bearing source
[*]Turn it to 0° relative, select "Bow" on the receiver selector and let the gyros synchronize
[*]Turn it to 180° relative, select "Stern" and let the stern gyro synchronize
[*]Leave the receiver selector on "None" until you attack
[/olist]
With the gyros pre-set and the receiver off while scanning, you avoid the stock system's constant gyro chasing and can fire quickly in both directions.
